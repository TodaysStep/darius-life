**MY EMERGENCIES ARE NOT YOUR EMERGENCIES.**

The status above is a personal status. It is not an incident report, a service notice, or a promise of availability.

When a site won't load or an app can't connect, that is usually the Internet's infrastructure, not news about me. If infrastructure is the problem, my attention goes to the infrastructure.

A technical outage does not create an obligation for me to explain myself. A personal update does not create a professional reporting relationship. A change in my work does not give anyone authority to reinterpret what I say. Someone else's emergency does not automatically become mine.

When something is down, I already know about it before anyone else tells me. That is when I am most unavailable. An outage is not a reason to contact me, and it does not make anyone's message more urgent.

My affiliations, projects, and work can change. My personal status space remains mine.
