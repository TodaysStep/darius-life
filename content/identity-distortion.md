I do not accept characterizations that recast who I am or what I intend into a role I did not choose. This includes being reversed, villainized, or costumed. Reports of this kind are preserved by the Private Records Office at villaincostume.com. The office preserves an account in the reporter's own words, attaches evidence without replacing the original account, and issues a receipt. A receipt proves only that a record with that incident number existed at a stated time under a stated policy version. It never proves that the account is true. The office does not adjudicate, determine guilt, or issue legal findings. Records are private by default. Only receipt-number verification is public.
